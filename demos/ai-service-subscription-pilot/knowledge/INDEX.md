# AI Service Subscription Pilot Knowledge Index

This directory stores demo-local findings and reproducible fixes. It is supporting knowledge, not product or provider authority.

## Routing

- `findings/`: investigated behavior, comparisons, open questions, and evidence gaps
- `fixes/`: reproducible symptom, root cause, bounded fix, and verification

## Entry Rules

- Link affected `REQ-*`, `DESIGN-*`, `SLICE-*`, `TASK-*`, `TC-*`, or `EVID-*` records.
- Cite the payment wiki and current official documentation for payment claims.
- Mark provider conclusions as documented, simulated, sandbox-proven, unsupported, or unresolved.
- Sanitize payloads and never store credentials, private customer data, or reusable tokens.
- Promote broadly reusable lessons through the repository `learnings/` process; do not duplicate them here.
